# KFOO visual monitoring rules — 2026-09-14

- Prioritize the continuity average as a trend-continuation context.
- Above the average is positive context; below is negative context; contact is treated as possible oscillation.
- A clean break requires the candle to open and close on the new side of the continuity average.
- Use Swing monitoring for primary-wave versus correction context.
- Use Lightning/Scalp monitoring for faster rebound/correction context.
- Treat the Risk Ratio bar as context, not as a standalone trade trigger.
- Keep screenshot candle monitoring to a small recent window (24 by default).
- Never infer OHLC or a trade solely from pixels.
- Keep execution OFF until independent verification gates pass.
